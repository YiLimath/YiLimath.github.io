I am a final-year PhD candidate at Wuhan University, supervised by [Sheng Rao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mKrV7coAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) and [Christopher D. Hacon](https://www.math.utah.edu/~hacon/), expecting to defend in December 2026. My research interests lie in **birational geometry** and **complex geometry**.

My recent work is on the minimal model program for Kähler varieties and what it yields for deformation problems. With Hacon and Rao I proved the deformation invariance of plurigenera for smooth families of Kähler threefolds, confirming Siu's conjecture in dimension three ([arXiv:2602.04158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04158)); with Hacon and Lingyao Xie I showed that flips and divisorial contractions preserve the Kähler condition for generalized klt pairs, together with a Kähler criterion for varieties in Fujiki's class $\mathcal{C}$ ([arXiv:2608.20588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20588)).

I use this website to collect research notes, organize the topics I am studying, and record some of the ideas and discoveries that appear along the way.

## Research Interests

Currently, I mainly work on:

- the Kähler minimal model program and its applications to deformation problems;
- the structure of Kähler Calabi–Yau varieties and varieties with nef anti-canonical divisors;
- boundedness and moduli aspects of birational geometry.
