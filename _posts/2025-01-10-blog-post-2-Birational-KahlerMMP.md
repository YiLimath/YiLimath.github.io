The aim of these notes is to introduce the minimal model program for Kähler varieties. Compared with the projective minimal model program, the Kähler case presents three major difficulties.

1. **Bend and break.** Mori's bend-and-break technique is not available for Kähler varieties, since it runs through reduction to positive characteristic.
2. **The base point free and cone theorems.** A Kähler manifold carrying a big line bundle is automatically projective, so on a genuinely non-projective Kähler manifold there are no big line bundles to work with, and the usual statements have to be replaced by transcendental ones.
3. **The contraction theorem.** In the classical MMP one appeals to the base point free theorem to produce a semi-ample divisor, and that divisor induces the contraction morphism. This route is not available in the Kähler setting.

> In general, we aim to study the following question: To what extent are natural geometric or sheaf-theoretic constructions on compact Kähler manifolds determined by analogous constructions in the projective setting? Further structural results could provide a general framework for reducing certain questions about Kähler manifolds to the algebraic setting.
