# Research record

## Exact target
ID, version, domain, assumptions, quantifiers, conclusion.

## Attempts
Keep original outputs in separate files. For each argument: prerequisites, actual calculation, remaining obligation.

## Evidence
Exact proof, source locator, counterexample, or actual tool output. Distinguish proposed checks from completed checks.

## Admission
Which statement is supported, by what evidence, for which version? Which dependencies remain assumptions?

## Revision
What changed? Which conclusions survive, require rechecking, or lose support?

## Next experiment
One action, its predicted outcome, and what a contrary result would teach us.
